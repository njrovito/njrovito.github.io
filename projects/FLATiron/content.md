[FLATiron](https://github.com/flowlabcu/FLATiron#) is a hierarchical, modular, finite element library for flow physics and transport phenomena developed based on research and development activities at FLOWLab at the University of Colorado Boulder. FLATiron (FLAT-FE) is named after the famous rock formations in Boulder, Colorado. The toolkit is based on the open-source finite element library, FEniCS, and leverages the underlying dolfinx and Unified Form Language (`ufl`) modules in its design.
